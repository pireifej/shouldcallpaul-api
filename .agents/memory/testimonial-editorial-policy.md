---
name: Testimonial editorial policy
description: Preserve source quotations, abbreviated attributions, and date precision when maintaining testimonials.
---

Treat supplied testimonial quotes as verbatim source material: do not polish
wording or punctuation. Keep abbreviated names abbreviated. Unknown dates stay
blank; a supplied year alone does not justify inventing a month or day. Preserve
verified dates when reconciling an existing record.

**Why:** The owner's frontend handoff explicitly requires exact quotes and
abbreviated names, and distinguishes separate testimonials from the same person
at different events. Name-only deduplication would discard legitimate entries.

**How to apply:** Reconcile using event context as well as attribution, keep
separate-event testimonials, and respect the owner's supplied group/order.
