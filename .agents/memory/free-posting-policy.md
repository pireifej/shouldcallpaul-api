---
name: Free posting baseline
description: User-confirmed posting policy during and after the subscription transition.
---

Keep one prayer request per day as the free baseline; do not temporarily unlock multiple daily requests while IAP is unfinished.

**Why:** The user explicitly confirmed that they like the single-request-per-day baseline and declined a temporary posting-limit relaxation.

**How to apply:** Preserve this baseline when completing the subscription transition. The temporary Daily Bread and Prayer Walk audio exceptions do not imply a posting-limit exception.

The shared free posting day resets at midnight `America/New_York` for all clients,
not at midnight UTC or in a device-selected timezone.

**Why:** The approved calendar-day correction addresses yesterday-evening Eastern
posts incorrectly consuming the next UTC day's allowance. This changes the day
boundary, not the free allowance or subscription policy.

**How to apply:** Treat Eastern as an app-wide product policy unless the user
explicitly changes it. Do not quietly switch to per-device days or a rolling
24-hour cooldown when changing posting or frontend quota messaging.
